# Week 3: Health and Lifecycles

## Session Goals

- Distinguish liveness, readiness, and startup probes.
- Make pods fail fast and recover safely.
- Add graceful shutdown behavior.

## Key Concepts

- Readiness controls traffic.
- Liveness controls restart behavior.
- Startup probes give slow apps time to initialize.

## Lab Focus

- Add probes to a deployment.
- Verify startup and termination behavior.
- Observe probe failures in pod status and events.

## Deliverable

Resilient YAML suite with health checks.
